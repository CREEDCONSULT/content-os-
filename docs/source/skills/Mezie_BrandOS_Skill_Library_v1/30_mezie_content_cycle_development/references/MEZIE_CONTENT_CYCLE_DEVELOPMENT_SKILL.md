# MEZIE CONTENT CYCLE DEVELOPMENT SKILL

## 1. PURPOSE

This skill governs the full development of a periodic content cycle for the Mezie brand.

It converts brand strategy, founder context, business activity, audience needs, creator intelligence, current developments, available evidence, production capacity, and platform requirements into a controlled system for producing ready-to-research, ready-to-script, ready-to-record, ready-to-edit, ready-to-publish, and measurable content.

This skill must be used whenever the Mezie BrandOS is asked to create, revise, extend, audit, or operationalize:

- A monthly content calendar
- A weekly publishing cycle
- A campaign calendar
- A product-launch content plan
- A founder-brand content sprint
- A quarterly editorial program
- A recurring content series
- A reactive content window
- A cross-platform repurposing plan
- A research-to-publication content pipeline
- A production batch plan
- A creator-benchmark-informed content strategy

This skill is not limited to August or any fixed month.

The August framework is the originating implementation, but this file converts it into a reusable BrandOS capability for all future cycles.

---

# 2. CORE OPERATING PRINCIPLE

The content cycle must not be treated as a list of posting dates.

It must be treated as a controlled founder-brand production system.

The governing sequence is:

```text
Brand Context
→ Capacity Lock
→ Research
→ Evidence Validation
→ Opportunity Mapping
→ Content Architecture
→ Idea Generation
→ Idea Scoring
→ Content Brief
→ Hook Development
→ Script
→ Fact Check
→ Creative Direction
→ Production Plan
→ Shoot
→ Edit
→ Platform Adaptation
→ Approval
→ Publish
→ Analytics Review
→ Repurpose
→ Learning Record
```

No content item may bypass the stages required for its level of factual, reputational, financial, legal, creative, or production risk.

# 7. MISSING-INPUT POLICY

Do not fabricate missing inputs.

When inputs are missing:

1. Use verified existing BrandOS context.
2. Use previously approved brand principles.
3. Mark assumptions explicitly.
4. Use conservative production estimates.
5. Separate confirmed facts from recommendations.
6. Create placeholders where exact project evidence is unavailable.
7. Do not invent creator names, performance results, audience data, client results, business milestones, or personal stories.
8. Do not state that research has been completed when it has not.
9. Do not state that a creator pattern is established without supporting evidence.
10. Do not produce a falsely precise calendar from incomplete inputs.

When the system can proceed responsibly with an assumption, label it:

- Confirmed
- Inferred
- Proposed
- Unverified
- Requires approval

# 8. TRUTH, EVIDENCE, AND NO-HALLUCINATION STANDARD

All content developed through this skill must comply with the following standard.

## 8.1 Prohibited Practices

Do not include:

- Fabricated personal experiences
- Invented outcomes
- False client results
- Unsupported market claims
- Fake urgency
- Fabricated quotations
- Invented statistics
- False creator analysis
- Fake trends
- Misrepresented research
- Unverified platform rules presented as fact
- Claims of authority beyond the documented authority ladder
- Financial guarantees
- Investment signals disguised as education
- Near-copying of another creator’s language or persona
- Statements that a conceptual system has been built when it has only been designed

## 8.2 Claim Classification

Every major claim must be classified internally as one of the following:

### Verified Fact

Supported by reliable evidence.

### Founder Experience

Based on a real event, project, decision, or observation from Mezie’s life or work.

### Founder Interpretation

Mezie’s reasoned view of available evidence.

### Working Hypothesis

A proposition being tested.

### Strategic Recommendation

A proposed course of action.

### Creative Device

A metaphor, narrative device, dramatization, or visual concept that must not misrepresent reality.

### Unverified Signal

An observation that may justify further research but cannot yet support a public factual claim.

## 8.3 Evidence Rule

Where evidence is required, prioritize:

1. Primary sources
2. Official product documentation
3. Government or regulatory sources
4. Original research
5. Company filings
6. Direct interviews
7. Project records
8. Internal implementation evidence
9. High-quality secondary analysis
10. Discovery sources for question generation only

Discovery sources such as creator posts, comments, Reddit, X, forums, and aggregators may be used to identify audience language, emerging questions, objections, or possible trends.

They may not alone establish a factual conclusion.

# 9. AUTHORITY LADDER

Every content item must be matched to the appropriate level of authority.

## Level 1 — Demonstrated Authority

Use when Mezie has direct evidence, implementation history, documented work, or lived experience.

Language may be decisive where the evidence supports it.

## Level 2 — Developing Authority

Use when Mezie has meaningful practice, research, or project exposure but is still building depth.

Language should remain confident but qualified.

## Level 3 — Exploratory Authority

Use when Mezie is investigating a subject, testing a hypothesis, learning publicly, or interpreting emerging information.

Language must clearly distinguish exploration from expertise.

## Authority Misalignment Rule

Do not write expert-level claims from exploratory evidence.

Do not weaken demonstrated authority with unnecessary vagueness.

Do not use artificial confidence to imitate high-authority creators.

# 10. CONTENT ARCHITECTURE

Each calendar item must be classified across the following layers.

## Layer 1 — Brand Pillar

What recurring territory does the idea belong to?

## Layer 2 — Series

What recognizable recurring format or editorial promise does it serve?

## Layer 3 — Content Mode

Does it teach, demonstrate, document, interpret, argue, reflect, or invite?

## Layer 4 — Production Format

How will the content be captured and presented?

## Layer 5 — Platform Adaptation

How will the idea be rewritten and reformatted for each platform?

## Layer 6 — Objective

What outcome is the content designed to produce?

## Layer 7 — Experiment

What content hypothesis is being tested?

No final calendar entry should exist without these classifications.

- Minimal talking head

The weekday structure is a governing rhythm, not a rigid prison.

A timely or strategically important content item may interrupt the rhythm when:

- It is verified
- It is brand relevant
- It is audience relevant
- It has a clear expiry date
- It has a distinct Mezie angle
- It is more valuable than the displaced scheduled item

# 18. TREND VALIDATION

A trend-led content item must contain:

- At least two independent credible signals
- A defined time range
- Evidence that it is more than one isolated post
- Brand relevance
- Audience relevance
- A distinct Mezie angle
- An expiry date
- A recommendation status

Do not call one creator post, one comment, one headline, or one product announcement a trend.

Use the term “signal” until evidence supports a broader conclusion.

# 24. SCRIPTING ARCHITECTURES

```text

3–10 seconds: Relevance
10–35 seconds: Development
```

# 41. FINAL GOVERNING STANDARD

The skill must continually enforce this principle:

> **Do not treat a content period as a collection of posting dates. Treat it as a controlled production cycle inside a living founder-brand operating system.**

Every cycle should move through:

> **Research → Evidence → Angle → Brief → Script → Direction → Production → Adaptation → Publication → Measurement → Learning.**

The desired result is not merely consistent posting.

The desired result is a recognizable, evidence-led, production-ready, platform-native, measurable content system that develops both:

- Mezie the founder, thinker, builder, and evolving individual
- Mezie the business operator, product developer, systems designer, creative director, and owner

The personal story makes the work human.

The business work makes the personal brand credible.

The system makes both sustainable.
